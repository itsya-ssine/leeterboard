# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The Leeterboard project aims to foster an open, welcoming, and inclusive community for competitive programmers, students, and software engineers. We are committed to providing a friendly and harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, gender identity, race, nationality, or personal beliefs.

## Expected Behavior

- **Be respectful and constructive:** Engage in thoughtful, courteous technical discussions and code reviews.
- **Collaborate with empathy:** Support newcomers and peers with patience and actionable feedback.
- **Respect privacy and security:** Adhere to our security guidelines; report any potential vulnerabilities privately.
- **Focus on community goals:** Keep discussions focused on learning, problem solving, and technical excellence.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Offensive comments, harassment, hate speech, or discriminatory language.
- Public or private intimidation, stalking, or inappropriate personal attention.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Disruptive conduct or trolling that undermines community collaboration.

## Reporting and Enforcement

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the repository maintainers. All reports will be reviewed promptly, treated confidentially, and resolved fairly. Maintainers reserve the right to remove comments, close issues, or suspend project access for violations of these guidelines.
